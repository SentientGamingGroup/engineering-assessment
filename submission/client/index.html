Connecting

Choose a beacon. Follow the pulse. Reveal the signal.

How to play
  1. Pick one of the three beacons — click it, or Tab to it and press Enter.
  2. Press Play. The relay charges while the server runs the round, then the pulse flies to the beacon the server selected.
  3. If it lands on your beacon, you score +1. The result always comes from the server — the pulse only reports it.

Pause freezes the presentation only; the round keeps running. Skip reveals the result as soon as it is ready. Replay re-watches your last result without playing a new round. New round returns to selection — your score stays. Refreshing or losing connection never loses a round in progress.
